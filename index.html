SAFETY GUIDE 01

🌎 Earthquake Safety

An earthquake is the sudden shaking of the ground caused by movements within the Earth's crust. It can happen without warning, making preparedness important.

BEFORE AN EARTHQUAKE

  • Know the designated evacuation areas and emergency exits.
  • Participate seriously in earthquake drills.
  • Identify safe spots such as sturdy tables or desks.
  • Keep emergency supplies accessible.
  • Secure heavy furniture and objects.

DURING AN EARTHQUAKE

  • Stay calm and avoid panicking.
  • Practice Drop, Cover, and Hold On.
  • Protect your head and neck.
  • Stay away from windows and falling objects.
  • Do not rush toward exits while the ground is shaking.

AFTER AN EARTHQUAKE

  • Wait until the shaking stops before evacuating.
  • Follow teachers and school authorities.
  • Use designated evacuation routes and stairs.
  • Stay away from damaged buildings and structures.
  • Be prepared for possible aftershocks.
REMEMBER: Drop, Cover, and Hold On. Follow your school's earthquake emergency plan.
SAFETY GUIDE 02

🔥 Fire Safety

Fire emergencies can spread quickly and create dangerous conditions. Awareness and proper evacuation procedures are essential in keeping everyone safe.

BEFORE A FIRE

  • Know the locations of emergency exits.
  • Learn your school's evacuation procedures.
  • Never play with matches, lighters, or electrical equipment.
  • Avoid overloading electrical outlets.
  • Participate in fire drills.

DURING A FIRE

  • Alert others and notify a teacher immediately.
  • Activate the fire alarm if instructed or appropriate.
  • Evacuate using the designated exit routes.
  • Use stairs instead of elevators.
  • Stay low if smoke is present.
  • Never return to the building to retrieve belongings.

AFTER A FIRE

  • Proceed to the designated assembly area.
  • Wait for teachers to account for students.
  • Follow instructions from firefighters and authorities.
  • Do not re-enter the building until declared safe.
  • Report injuries or missing persons to authorities.
REMEMBER: Leave immediately when instructed. Your safety is more important than your belongings.
SAFETY GUIDE 03

⚠️ Bomb Threat Safety

A bomb threat is a warning or claim that an explosive device may be present in a location. Every threat must be treated seriously and reported to the proper authorities.

BEFORE A THREAT

  • Know your school's emergency procedures.
  • Be familiar with designated assembly areas.
  • Report suspicious messages or threats to school authorities.
  • Understand how emergency announcements are communicated.

DURING A BOMB THREAT

  • Remain calm and take every threat seriously.
  • Immediately inform a teacher or school administrator.
  • Do not touch, move, or investigate suspicious objects.
  • Follow evacuation or shelter instructions from authorities.
  • Do not spread unverified information or rumors.

AFTER A BOMB THREAT

  • Remain in the designated safe area.
  • Wait for official clearance before returning.
  • Follow instructions from emergency responders.
  • Report any relevant information to authorities.
  • Do not share unverified reports on social media.
REMEMBER: Never investigate a suspicious object yourself. Leave the assessment to trained authorities.
SAFETY GUIDE 04

🛡️ School Shooting Safety

A school shooting is an emergency involving gunfire within or near a school. Although uncommon, knowing basic protective actions and following school protocols are important.

BEFORE AN EMERGENCY

  • Know your school's emergency response procedures.
  • Pay attention to safety orientations and drills.
  • Know how teachers communicate emergency announcements.
  • Report serious threats or concerning behavior to trusted adults.
  • Know how to contact school authorities.

DURING AN EMERGENCY

  • Follow the school's emergency instructions immediately.
  • If a safe route away from danger is available, move away.
  • If leaving is unsafe, follow the school's shelter-in-place procedure.
  • Stay quiet and follow instructions from authorities.
  • Do not approach or investigate the source of danger.
  • Contact emergency services when it is safe to do so.

AFTER AN EMERGENCY

  • Wait for official instructions and clearance.
  • Follow directions from emergency responders.
  • Stay with your teacher or designated group when safe.
  • Report injuries or missing persons to authorities.
  • Seek support from trusted adults if feeling distressed.
REMEMBER: Your safety comes first. Follow official school emergency procedures and instructions from trained responders.
